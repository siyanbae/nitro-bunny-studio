Privacy Policy

소설 리더 - TXT·TTS / Novel Reader - TXT & TTS

Last reviewed: 2026-10-25 · Effective date: 2026-10-25

1. Scope

This policy applies to Nitro Bunny Studio's Android app 소설 리더 - TXT·TTS / Novel Reader - TXT & TTS (com.siyan.novel_reader). The app is an offline-first tool for reading local TXT files you select and listening with Android text-to-speech. It has no user accounts or developer-operated analytics or crash collection service.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in app-specific storage to provide its features:

  • Selected TXT content URIs, display names, filenames, sizes, modification times, and character encodings
  • Reading positions, progress, bookmarks, and short surrounding text
  • A normalized, parsed cache of TXT contents for faster reopening
  • App language and theme, shared reading and TTS preferences, and lock-screen preview settings
  • Advertising privacy choices stored by Google UMP

Original TXT files are read-only and are never edited or deleted. Document contents, filenames, URIs, search terms, and bookmark excerpts are not sent to a developer server or in advertising requests. See below for processing by the speech engine you choose.

3. Information processed by advertising services

Library and settings banners use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may automatically collect and share the following for advertising, performance measurement, fraud prevention, and security:

  • IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, and ad views
  • Diagnostics, such as app/SDK runtime, interruptions, and energy use
  • Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and applicable device- or account-related identifiers

This information is encrypted in transit using TLS. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. UMP consent messages appear according to your region and applicable requirements; where required, privacy options in app settings let you manage your choices. Local TXT reading remains available regardless of consent or ad loading failures. Google's processing is governed by the policies below and applicable advertising terms.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses advertising technologies

4. Permissions and speech engines

  • Files: only read access granted by the system file picker is used; broad storage permission is not requested.
  • Notifications and a foreground media playback service: used for playback controls and continuous speech with the screen off.
  • Internet and network state: used for AdMob/UMP requests. Advertising identifiers may be used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

The app passes text to your selected Android TTS engine for speech. Offline voices process it on the device; if you choose a network voice, the engine provider may process text externally. That provider's policy governs processing and retention. Choose an offline voice to listen without sending document text off the device.

5. Retention and deletion

Local records and settings remain until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Android may clear the parsed cache earlier. Removing a recent document deletes its app records and parsed cache, while preserving the source TXT. Resetting settings resets only global preferences and preserves documents, reading positions, and bookmarks. Google policies and your Google/Android ad settings govern AdMob/UMP retention and deletion. There are no user accounts. Contact the email below with privacy questions or requests for deletion instructions.

6. Children's privacy

The app is intended for general readers aged 13 and older. It is not designed or marketed for children under 13. Please contact us below with concerns or questions about children's personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

If data categories, SDKs, or processing purposes change, we update this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures together. Significant changes are communicated through reasonable means, such as app update notices.

2. 앱이 기기에 저장하는 정보

앱은 기능 제공을 위해 다음 정보를 앱 전용 저장소에 보관합니다.

  • 앱 언어·테마, 전체 TXT 공통 읽기·TTS 설정과 잠금 화면 미리보기 설정
  • Google UMP가 저장하는 광고 개인정보 선택 상태

원본 TXT는 읽기 전용으로 취급하며 수정하거나 삭제하지 않습니다. 문서 내용, 파일명, URI, 검색어와 북마크 발췌는 개발자 서버나 광고 요청으로 전송하지 않습니다. 사용자가 선택한 음성 엔진의 처리는 아래를 참고해 주세요.

  • 앱·SDK 실행 시간, 중단, 에너지 사용량 등 진단 정보
  • Android 광고 ID, App Set ID와 적용 가능한 기기·계정 관련 식별자

이 정보는 전송 중 TLS로 암호화됩니다. Android 설정에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다. 지역과 관련 법령에 따라 UMP 동의 화면이 표시되고, 필요한 경우 앱 설정의 개인정보 옵션에서 선택을 관리할 수 있습니다. 동의 여부나 광고 로드 실패와 관계없이 로컬 TXT 읽기를 이용할 수 있습니다. Google의 처리에는 아래 정책과 관련 광고 약관이 적용됩니다.

2. 端末に保存する情報

機能の提供のため、アプリ専用ストレージに以下を保存します。

  • 選択したTXTのcontent URI、表示名、ファイル名、サイズ、更新日時、文字エンコーディング
  • 読書位置、進捗、ブックマークと短い前後のテキスト

元のTXTは読み取り専用で、編集・削除しません。本文、ファイル名、URI、検索語、ブックマークの抜粋を開発者サーバーや広告リクエストに送信しません。選択した音声エンジンによる処理は下記をご覧ください。

3. 広告サービスが処理する情報

ライブラリと設定画面のバナーには、Google Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob）とGoogle User Messaging Platform（UMP）を使用します。Googleは広告配信、効果測定、不正防止、セキュリティのため、以下を自動的に収集・共有することがあります。

  • IPアドレスと、それから推定したおおよその位置

  • アプリ・SDKの実行時間、中断、電力使用量などの診断情報
  • Android広告ID、App Set ID、該当する端末・アカウント関連の識別子

これらの情報はTLSにより転送中に暗号化されます。広告IDはAndroid設定でリセット・削除できます。地域や適用要件に応じてUMP同意画面が表示され、必要な場合はアプリ設定のプライバシーオプションで選択を管理できます。同意の有無や広告読み込みの失敗にかかわらず、ローカルTXTを読むことができます。Googleによる処理には下記の方針と関連する広告規約が適用されます。

  • ファイル：システムのファイル選択画面で付与された読み取り権限のみを使用し、広範なストレージ権限は要求しません。

ローカル記録と設定は、アプリデータの消去またはアンインストールまで保存されます。解析キャッシュはAndroidが先に消去する場合があります。最近の文書から項目を削除すると、アプリ内記録と解析キャッシュが削除され、元のTXTは残ります。設定のリセットは全体設定のみを初期化し、文書、読書位置、ブックマークは保持します。AdMob/UMPデータの保存・削除はGoogleの方針とGoogle/Android広告設定に従います。ユーザーアカウントはありません。プライバシーや削除方法に関するお問い合わせは下記のメールで受け付けます。
